Fish Alchemy: Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026. Applies to the Android game "Fish Alchemy: Breed Rare Fish" (Vietnamese name "Bể Lai"), package tev.dating.helper. In this policy "we" means the independent developer of the game; contact: tev.pham153@gmail.com.

In short

  • The game has no account, no sign-in and no chat. We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts or location.
  • Your game progress is stored on your phone. Android may back it up to your own Google account (Auto Backup).
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads (Google AdMob). Advertising and analytics SDKs may use your device's advertising ID.
  • The game is for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13.

2. Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. Ads are served by Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions, Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads load; you can change it any time in Settings. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads).

3. Analytics and crash reports

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collect the events and crash reports described above. Firebase may use a random app-instance ID and the advertising ID. These services are operated by Google and process data under their own terms.

4. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us data (for example through feedback), write to us and we will delete it.

5. Retention, deletion and your rights

  • Game save: it lives on your phone. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it. To delete the Auto Backup copy, use Google Drive backup settings on your phone or your Google account.
  • No account: the game has no account to delete.
  • Analytics, crash and ad data: collected by Google under its policies. Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalisation in Android settings. Uninstalling the game stops new data.
  • Feedback: reports carry no account or e-mail, so we cannot find yours by identity. To have one deleted, e-mail us the approximate time you sent it and the text, and we will delete it. Otherwise we keep feedback only as long as needed to fix the problem it describes.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, Vietnam or California) you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Write to tev.pham153@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

6. Security and transfers

Feedback and SDK traffic use HTTPS. Google processes data in countries that may differ from yours, under the safeguards described in its policies.

7. Changes

If we change what the game collects, we update this page and its date before the new version is released.
